    Shopping list

    Lights and garland

    1. Inside lights

    • Tie lights to the inside face of the legs so the inside glows and light shines out through the garland.
    • Below the crosspiece, go back and forth: around the five closed sides, up 6", and back.
    • Above the crosspiece, spiral all the way around.
    • Zip-tie at every leg. Start near your outlet and check how many strands can plug end to end.

    2. Garland

    • Base layer: thin pine garland with a bendable wire center, wrapped bottom up in rows about 5" apart. The wire core keeps each strand straight between legs.
    • Tie at every leg, and once mid-span to the light wire behind it so long spans don't sag.
    • Fullness layer: wide garland with long posable tips, wrapped over the base about 12" apart. Bend the tips out and slightly down, like real boughs; fill thin spots with wired pine picks.
    • Use reusable twist ties so nothing needs cutting at takedown.
    • Run strands up each door leg and along the crosspiece for a clean doorway.

    3. Outside lights

    • Weave a second set through the garland, tucking bulbs into the branches for depth.
    • Bend garland tips over the wires to hide them.

    4. Disco ball (optional)

    • The hub has an eye-bolt mount underneath: a ¼"-20 nut slides into a side slot and the eye bolt threads into it. Hang a small ball on fishing line or an S-hook.
    • Aim a small battery spotlight at it from a base corner; without a focused light it won't sparkle.

    Before you print

    1. Cut a short piece off one board and sand the edges lightly.
    2. Build and print the three fit-test rings. They're 0.5 mm apart and marked with 1, 2 and 3 notches.
    3. Slide each onto the strip. The right one goes on by hand with light friction and doesn't wobble.
    4. Enter that ring's size in Socket size, set your other measurements, and build the full set.
    5. Paint the strips before fitting, or leave the last 2" of each end bare: paint adds thickness.

    Assembly

    1. Build the U upside down: four 2-arm corners, two doorway corners, five base pieces, one flat-head screw up through the bottom of each arm. Flip it over.
    2. Slide the nylon lock nut into the slot on the side of the hub's boss and thread the disco ball eye bolt up into it, then press in the star post.
    3. Slide a crosspiece sleeve onto each front leg before standing them up.
    4. Drop three legs into alternating corners, lean them together, and press the hub onto their tops from a ladder.
    5. Add the other three legs: foot first, then tip the top into its socket.
    6. Slide the sleeves to door height, measure between them, cut the crosspiece, set it in, and screw the sleeves.
    7. Lights, garland, star, disco ball.

    Safety: LED lights only. Push on the finished frame from every direction before decorating, and don't let anyone climb it. Keep cords tucked behind a base corner, away from pets and small hands.
