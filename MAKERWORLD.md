## Title

Walk-In Teepee Christmas Tree – Printed Connectors (Customizable)

## Summary

Printed joints for a six-legged, kid-sized walk-in tree frame. Every wood cut is square, the parts print with no supports, and the free planner sizes them to your space.

## Description

Build your own walk-in Christmas tree. Six 1½" square wood legs, a U-shaped base, a door crosspiece and 10 printed connectors make a frame you cover in garland and lights. Inside, it's a reading nook for kids.

**Make it fit your space**
- **Free planner:** https://lacyrohde.github.io/christmas-teepee/. Enter leg length, base width, door height, ceiling height and socket size. You get a 3D preview, the wood cut list, a shopping list and matching STL files, built in your browser.
- **Customize button:** change leg length, base width and socket size here on MakerWorld.
- **Source and command-line generator:** https://github.com/lacyrohde/christmas-teepee

**This print profile is my build:** 96¼" legs (my "8 ft" boards), a 5 ft base, crosspiece at 40", 9 ft ceiling, 38.6 mm sockets. The frame stands about 7' 9", and a 10" star tops out around 8' 10". The doorway is about 27½" wide at the floor.

**Print the fit-test rings first.** Lumber varies. Slide the three rings onto your wood; the one that fits snugly by hand is your socket size. If it isn't 38.6 mm, generate your own set with the planner or Customize.

**No supports needed.** Every socket that lies sideways has a sloped roof above the square part the wood sits in, topped with a short flat bridge, so it holds itself up as it prints. The crosspiece sleeves have a solid foot under the side socket.

**What's printed (10 parts, about 1.2 kg PETG)**
- Top hub ×1: holds the six legs and the star post, with an eye-bolt mount underneath for a disco ball
- Star post ×1: a cone most star toppers grip
- Base corner ×4
- Doorway corner ×2 (left and right)
- Crosspiece sleeve ×2 (left and right)

**You'll also need**
- Wood: 8 boards, 1½" × 1½" × 96¼" (sold as 2×2s)
  - 6 legs at full length, no cuts
  - 5 base pieces at 27"
  - 1 door crosspiece, about 15¼" (cut last, to fit between the sleeves)
- 10 × #8 × 1¼" flat-head wood screws for the base, plus 2 more for the sleeves
- Optional disco ball mount: ¼"-20 × 1" eye bolt and a ¼"-20 nylon lock nut (it slides into a slot in the hub; no washer)
- Base garland: about 140 ft of thin pine garland with a bendable wire center (17 × 9 ft strands, 220+ tips each), wrapped in rows about 5" apart
- Fullness garland: about 60 ft of wide garland, 12–16", with long posable tips (7 × 9 ft strands), wrapped over the base about 12" apart
- LED lights and a star topper

**Assembly**
1. Build the U-shaped base upside down: corners plus five base pieces, one flat-head screw up through the bottom of each arm. Flip it over.
2. If you're hanging a disco ball, slide the nut into the slot under the hub and thread the eye bolt into it. Then press in the star post.
3. Slide the sleeves onto the two front legs before standing them up.
4. Stand three legs in alternating corners, lean them together, and press the hub on from a ladder. Add the other three.
5. Set the sleeves at door height, cut the crosspiece to fit, and screw the sleeves.
6. Lights, garland, star.

**Safety:** LED lights only. Push on the frame from every direction before decorating, and don't let anyone climb it.

## Print profile

**Name:** 0.20mm Standard – PETG – X2D – 96¼" legs, 5 ft base, 38.6 mm sockets

**Description:** All 10 parts on 4 plates, no supports. 0.20mm Standard with Arachne walls, 4 walls, 4 top and bottom layers and 20% gyroid infill (15% on the base parts); 10 mm brim on the star post so the tall, narrow post stays put as it prints. Sockets are 38.6 mm: print the fit-test rings first and use the planner or Customize if your wood needs a different size.
